# Permission scope review

A proposed additional control made a manifest CURRENT, then injected denial at
the external ancestor and requested a canonical freshness repair. The repair
completed. This was not evidence of swallowed PermissionError: that operation
never requested the denied external metadata. Canonical plan writes already
use an explicit ownership root; the missing-manifest writer uses the complete
ancestor proof. Those are different required resources.

Requiring a new external-ancestor probe for every canonical repair would expand
the permission contract without evidence and needlessly block authorized root
operations. The proposed assertion was rejected on that independent ground and
removed. The frozen 21-case oracle and production subjects remain byte-identical
to their recorded pair. A real manifest permission failure remains a hard refusal
before canonical repair; no ancestor proof needed by the manifest writer was
removed or bypassed.
