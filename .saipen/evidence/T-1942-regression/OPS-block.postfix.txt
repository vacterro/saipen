Pending cumulative-batch reconciliation (T-1839) stops a later reviewed wave
stranding earlier pending cohorts on obsolete shared-file hashes.
`saipen cohort reconcile C-FINAL --from C-OLD[,C-OLD] --review-work T-N` reuses
PLAN/APPLY, ownership, Source, verification and release owners. Every cohort must
be pending and locally complete under its original owner; T-N must be finished,
verified, a C-FINAL member and own its whole scope with inherited paths. Old
hashes, BOARD rows and finishes stay unchanged. One journal-bound immutable
receipt names the original cohorts and reviewed target; the old cohorts name it
as publication successor and all membership freezes. Missing coverage,
stale/unreviewed bytes, foreign owners, existing/nested links, shipped cohorts,
corruption and conflicting retries refuse without writes; exact retry and dry-run
write nothing. Inherited publication resolves only once the target holds a
matching terminal release receipt; the ordinary final-generation evidence,
staging and release gates still own publication.
