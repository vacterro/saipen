## Why not give both agents the same unified writable task?

Because SAIPEN is the producer/source of truth and SAIPATCH is the consumer.
If both independently implement the same cross-repo spec at once, they can silently invent different meanings for COMPLETE, WAIT, audit epochs, or closure.

Contract first. Consumer second.
