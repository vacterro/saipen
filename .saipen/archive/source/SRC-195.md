ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION

Can SAIPEN safely distinguish:

1. GLOBAL_UNSAFE
   Work must stop because an integrity or safety
   invariant affecting the operation is violated.

2. WORK_BOUNDARY_BLOCKED
   A specific ticket or operation is blocked,
   without automatically blocking unrelated work.

3. EXTERNAL_ACCEPTANCE_PENDING
   Operator-owned evidence or external approval
   remains outstanding.

4. INDEPENDENT_WORK_ADMISSIBLE
   Unrelated work may proceed only when the
   canonical authority explicitly permits it.

5. REPAIR_REQUIRED
   The failure is actionable, but an authorized
   executable remediation path is missing.

These are proposed conceptual distinctions,
not permission to introduce new protocol states
without compatibility and governance review.
